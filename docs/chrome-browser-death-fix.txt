Chrome Browser Death on Headless VMs — Root Cause, Fixes, and Current Status
=============================================================================
(rewritten 2026-08-24; the previous version of this file described two fixes
and a 1024 MB heap cap, which had drifted badly from reality — that drift is
why the video-teardown behaviour below went unnoticed for a month)

THE ORIGINAL INCIDENT (Feb 2026, on the OLD 8-core GPU-less Hetzner VM)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Three compounding factors made the Chrome renderer unresponsive after 19-95
jobs:

1. Software video decoding — the VM had no GPU, so YouTube's VP9/AV1 was
   decoded on CPU, saturating the renderer main thread.   [hardware-dependent]
2. V8 heap bloat — a ChromeDriver leak (Selenium #13510/#13533) grew the heap
   to 4GB+ across SPA navigations.                        [hardware-INdependent]
3. GC thrashing at ~4GB heap left no CPU for WebDriver commands.

WHAT WAS ACTUALLY DEPLOYED (as of 2026-08, in primemover_runner)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Not two fixes but three H.264 injection vectors plus two side effects,
accumulated over commits e8a80e1, 4d44084, 70e55eb:

a) MAIN-world extension (config/h264-override-extension/), loaded into every
   MLX profile via ProfileService.java. On YouTube this is the ONLY effective
   vector. It also carried an UNDOCUMENTED side effect: it paused, muted and
   unloaded the <video> element 3s after every navigation ("free decoder
   buffers"). See MEASUREMENT below.
b) CDP Page.addScriptToEvaluateOnNewDocument (TaskOperator.java) — BROKEN in
   production: Selenium has no CDP mapping for Mimic 151, fails every session.
c) Post-load JS (VisitJobHandler.java) — always no-ops on YouTube (the
   extension wins the document_start race); leaks the override onto
   non-YouTube pages.
d) V8 heap cap: --max-old-space-size=512 effective (ProfileService.java);
   the 1024 in DefaultMultiLoginProfile.json is a stale template value.

MEASUREMENT OF THE VIDEO TEARDOWN (2026-08-24, production stack, A/B)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Instrumented extension, MLX/Mimic browser, one full YouTube e2e per arm,
video element sampled in-page, data recovered from per-job localstorage
captures in MinIO:

  WITH teardown:  playback starts ~2.5s after the watch page loads; the
                  teardown kills it at +3s (readyState 0, currentTime 0,
                  muted); dead ~15-20s; YouTube SELF-RECOVERS: resumes,
                  unmuted, playhead advancing (5.0s -> 25.0s over 20s).
                  Killed again after every SPA navigation.
  WITHOUT:        continuous playback from +3s, never muted.

So bots DO watch video (the recovery is why player telemetry showed
streaming), but each navigation loses its first ~15-20s — on 30s dwells,
roughly two thirds of the intended watch signal. Removal PR:
primemover_runner #54. Only one non-test experiment ran while the teardown
was live: experiment 42 (2026-08-12..18); its arms were affected uniformly.

CURRENT STATUS ON THE NEW HOST (ex63: 20 cores, 62 GB, Arrow Lake iGPU)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Cause 1 is GONE as of 2026-08-22: VA-API hardware decode works end to end
(intel-media-va-driver-non-free 26.1.2; primemover in BOTH the render and
video groups; services restarted so they hold the groups; verified by the
Mimic gpu-process holding /dev/dri/renderD128 during a real e2e).
Measured with hardware decode live, full 20-job YouTube run:
  H.264 override only: 67 CPU-s, peak browser RSS 1.9 GB
  no override at all:  70 CPU-s, peak browser RSS 1.9 GB
=> the codec override no longer buys performance. It is retained for now so
the negotiated codec does not change mid-study; retire it at an experiment
boundary, together with vectors (b) and (c).

Causes 2-3 are a ChromeDriver bug and are NOT fixed by hardware: keep the
V8 heap cap (d) regardless of host.

OPERATIONAL NOTES
-----------------
- Supplementary groups are read at process start: after changing group
  membership, restart primemover-runner AND multilogin-desktop.
- Do NOT add GL flags (--use-gl=egl etc.) to the MLX profile: Chrome 151
  allows only (gl=egl-angle, angle=default); anything else kills the GPU
  process outright.
- scripts/monitor-memory.sh watches renderer RSS to catch regressions.
